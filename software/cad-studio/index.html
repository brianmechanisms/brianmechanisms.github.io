Software · CAD Studio

CAD Studio

CAD in the browser, built for engineering teams.

CAD Studio is a browser-based workspace for opening, inspecting and sharing 3D engineering models. Work with designs, drawings, bills of materials, motion studies and manufacturing files in one place, without installing CAD software.

Who it is for

Engineering Teams

Share models with engineers and reviewers across your organisation.

Workshops

Receive drawings and manufacturing files together in one download, ready for workshop use.

Researchers and Students

Share and review CAD models without installing software on every computer.

What it does

Model Inspection

Open GLB, STEP and STL files from your computer, a cloud drive or a git repository. Inspect assembly trees, hide or isolate parts, use ghost views and section or exploded views, measure geometry and inspect part properties.

Parts and Configurations

Manage part numbers, bills of materials and multiple configurations of the same product. Export bills of materials for further use.

Drawings and Manufacturing Packs

Get a drawing sheet for each part and download the drawings alongside STEP, STL and DXF files in one pack. Keep the files together for workshop use.

Motion and Simulation

Add motors to parts, define gear mates and play mechanisms on a timeline. Record motion studies as video, render the current view and inspect finite-element results displayed as colour maps on parts.

Electronics and Documentation

View electronics boards in a PCB view. Render product manuals and technical papers to PDF or Word alongside the design.

Automated Build Jobs

Run a design's own scripts to rebuild its models on your organisation's own machine. Return the results to the library for review.

Access

CAD Studio is currently available by request. Anyone can ask for access, and each new organisation is approved individually. Members access the application at cadstudio.cseco.co.ke.
