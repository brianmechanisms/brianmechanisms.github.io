Retail

PharaaS

Automate your shop.

Pharaas is a robotic system that automates a retail shop. It combines robotics with remote-operations software so customers can order and pay for products while the owner operates and monitors the shop remotely — an unmanned shop on your street, run from a phone.

Identity

Sojaly

Digital identity and verification for organisations and applications.

Sojaly gives organisations and applications a trusted way to identify people and verify the specific facts they need — from sign-in and age to location and presence. It covers membership credentials, attendance, visitor management, elections, digital receipts and chain of custody.

Measurement

Metriq

Measurement you can defend.

Metriq builds tamper-evident weighing scales and dispensers with server-issued, single-use calibration codes — so a weight or a volume holds up in court, at audit, and at the buying point.

More about Metriq →

Infrastructure

MetroTel International

Engineering and IT for connected infrastructure.

MetroTel International designs, deploys and supports intelligent, connected and automated systems for infrastructure and the built environment — smart metering, IoT and connectivity, automation and control, and the software platforms behind them — and advises clients independently where expertise rather than equipment is needed.
