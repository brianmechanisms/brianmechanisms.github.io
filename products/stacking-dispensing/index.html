Stacking & dispensing systems

Machines that store, stack and hand over goods on their own.

The SDS family (SDS-45 and SDS-90) stacks goods compactly and dispenses them one at a time on demand. Paired with ordering and payment software, an SDS turns a shelf of products into a self-service point that runs without a person standing next to it.

These are the machines behind Pharaas — our robotic retail company — and they work anywhere goods need to be stored densely and handed over reliably.

Talk to us about StackingThe research behind it

Key features

  • ▪Dense stacking with single-item dispensing
  • ▪45° and 90° configurations for different footprints
  • ▪Integrates with ordering, payment and remote-monitoring software
  • ▪Mechanically simple — few actuators, few failure points

Where to use it

Unmanned retail

A shop that sells while the owner is elsewhere — customers order and pay, the machine dispenses.

Pharmacies & controlled stock

Dispense counted items with a record of every handover.

Warehouses & stores

Buffer storage that feeds items out one at a time to a line or a counter.
